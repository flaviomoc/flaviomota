My work focuses on understanding how environmental change reshapes biodiversity across space and time. My research combines ecological niche modelling, macroecology, spatial analysis and biodiversity metrics to investigate species distributions, community structure and conservation priorities.

I have worked across questions involving habitat loss and fragmentation, climate-change impacts on birds and broader biodiversity, as well as ecological responses in human-impacted landscapes such as the Rio Doce basin.

The academic trajectory documented in the CV includes a PhD in Ecology and Biodiversity Conservation, a postdoctoral period, research positions and teaching activities in ecology and ecological niche modelling.
